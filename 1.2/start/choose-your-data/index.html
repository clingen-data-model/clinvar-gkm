Choose Your Data¶

ClinVar-GKM publishes the same data in several forms. They differ in size, in how often they change, and in what they are good for. This page compares them, with their current sizes, and suggests where to start.

Sizes are from the line's index.json and Parquet manifest.json for the 2026-11-15 release. A full bundle grows by a few MB a week; Check the current sizes below reads them live.

The options¶

Data Size Updated Good for Where
Published Parquet tables 20 tables, 5 KB to 1.7 GB each, 4.6 GB in all; most questions need two or three (sizes) Every weekly release; one set, overwritten SQL in DuckDB, Python, R or Spark; the browser console; questions about the current data Parquet Files
Weekly full bundle 4.9 GB compressed, 68.5 GB decompressed Every weekly release; kept for the current month only The newest state as JSON; loading records into the GA4GH libraries Weekly Full Bundles
Official monthly full bundle 4.9 GB compressed, 68.5 GB decompressed Once a month, lined up with ClinVar's monthly release; kept for good A fixed snapshot to cite or reproduce; the starting point for deltas Monthly Full Bundles
Monthly plus weekly deltas One monthly (4.9 GB), then one delta a week: usually 2 to 30 MB, up to about 150 MB Every weekly release Keeping a local copy current without downloading 4.9 GB a week; seeing exactly what changed each week Weekly Deltas, Keep Your Data Current
Previous releases About 4.8 GB per monthly; deltas as above Frozen: never change Data in an older schema (1.1.x, 1.0.1), for work that was built on it Previous Releases
Build your own Your choice: from a mini-bundle of one variant or one submitter to every table of a release Whenever you run it Parquet for an earlier release; a subset of ClinVar as a self-contained bundle; your own column set Build Your Own Dataset

Where to start: query the published Parquet. The Quick Start runs SQL in your browser with nothing to download; after that, download the two or three tables your question needs. To see the JSON format, download a weekly delta (a few MB) before a full bundle.

When you need a full bundle: use the newest weekly full for the current state, and the official monthly for a snapshot that matches ClinVar's monthly release. The monthly reflects the last release of the previous month, not the current week; How Releases Work explains the schedule.

A full bundle is too large to open whole

Decompressed, a full bundle is 68.5 GB. Read it streamed (gunzip -c), one record per line, rather than loading it into memory or decompressing it to disk; see Count records in a full bundle. Set Up Your Tools lists disk and memory needs.

Check the current sizes¶

These read the live index files. They need curl and jq (Set Up Your Tools).

# Every Parquet table: file, size in bytes, rows
curl -fsS https://data.clinvar-gkm.org/v1/1.2/parquet/manifest.json \
  | jq -r '.tables[] | [.file, .size, .rows] | @tsv'

# The full bundles of the current line, monthly and weekly: path, size in bytes
curl -fsS https://data.clinvar-gkm.org/v1/1.2/index.json \
  | jq -r '(.datasets.monthly[], .datasets.weekly[]) | [.path, .size] | @tsv'

# This year's weekly deltas: release, size in bytes
curl -fsS https://data.clinvar-gkm.org/v1/1.2/index.json \
  | jq -r '.deltas[] | [.release, .bundle.size] | @tsv'
