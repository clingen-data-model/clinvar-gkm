0008 — Bundles are the public record: immutability, archives, version lines and download metrics¶

  • Status: Proposed
  • Date: 2026-10-18
  • Plan: docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-bundle-record-and-metrics.md
  • Amends: ADR 0004 §3 (no live window: a superseded line freezes at the switch-over and is kept indefinitely, with no EOL date) and §5 (the in-place exception ends with 1.1.0); ADR 0006 (on PR #146) consequences (advance notice, (g)).
  • Amended: 2026-10-20 by ADR 0009 (R2 layout mirrors NCBI ClinVar): (a) weekly fulls are an exception to immutability; (c) archives are archive/YYYY/ inside a line, and the 1.0.1 generation becomes the frozen line v1/1.0/; (e) one overwritten Parquet dataset, none in archives.
  • Decided: 2026-10-21 (maintainer): 1.1.0 is GA (a); superseded lines are frozen and kept indefinitely, status frozen, with no eol, no deprecated status and no deletion window (b); a content-only correction that rewrites bundles opens a new MINOR line (c, ADR 0009 §5). Still open: N in (f).
  • Amended: 2026-10-21 to match the code merged on 1.0 (PR #182, r2_layout.py): the finished month's newest weekly full is kept until the new month's monthly is published (a); the switch-over notice uses status: "frozen" and superseded_by as the root and line index.json publish them (b); the Parquet manifest fields (e).

Context¶

Every corrective release so far has rewritten the published files under /v1/ in place: the 1.0.0 re-release, 1.0.1, and next the 1.1.0 republish (ADR 0006, on #146). Each rewrite replaced the bytes behind the same URLs. Anyone who downloaded an earlier generation now holds files that no longer exist anywhere, and nothing tells them which generation they have.

Before deciding how much machinery that problem deserves, we need to know whether anyone is downloading the data. What we know today:

  • Analytics exist only from 2026-10-14. Cloudflare GraphQL Analytics (httpRequestsAdaptiveGroups, per path, Free plan) covers data.clinvar-gkm.org from the day it started serving (ADR 0005). The zone rejects queries older than "4w3d", so it keeps about 31 days. Earlier traffic went to the r2.dev URL, which has no per-path analytics and is now disabled.

  • No complete full-bundle download so far. From 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-18, excluding our own IP:

    Path Requests Clients Served Reading
    v1/datasets/clinvar-gkm_00-latest.json.gz 18 8 IP/UA pairs 1.09 GB about 0.2 of one 4.8 GB full: at most a partial download
    dated monthly fulls (each) 2–4 — 0 bytes HEAD requests or link checks
    v1/deltas/00-latest/… delta 12 — 0.03 GB about 1–2 delta downloads
    old flat-root /datasets/… 7 — 0 bytes stale links

  • Parquet is not yet stable. The Parquet tables are re-derived from the BigQuery dict tables by separate SQL (ADR 0007, Workstream 1), and their layout changed in 1.0.1 and changes again in 1.1.0. The JSON bundles are the canonical output (ADR 0007, principle 2).

  • The 1.0.1 bundles are being archived to archive/1.0.1/ (the v1/ paths mirrored below it), with a MANIFEST.json (sha256 per file) and a README.txt, before the 1.1.0 republish rewrites /v1/. Under ADR 0009 this generation moves to the frozen line folder v1/1.0/.
  • No subscribers yet. The email list clinvar-gkm@broadinstitute.org has no members. When this ADR was proposed the schema had not been declared GA; the maintainer declared 1.1.0 GA on 2026-10-21 (a).

Decision¶

(a) The JSON bundles are the public record¶

The monthly full and delta JSON bundles are the published record of ClinVar-GKM. From the 1.1.0 republish on, a published bundle file is immutable: the bytes behind a published bundle path are never replaced. A correction is published into a new line folder (c), and the folder it replaces is frozen with its bytes intact. The mutable pointers are not records: index.json, the root manifest, feed.xml and every 00-latest path keep moving to the newest release.

Two amendments from ADR 0009:

  • Weekly full bundles are not records. The current month's weekly fulls under weekly/ are a convenience copy and are deleted at month rollover, as NCBI deletes its weekly releases. The finished month's newest weekly full is kept until the new month's official monthly, a server-side copy of it, is published, and is deleted then. This is a deliberate exception to immutability. The records are the official monthlies and the deltas, from which any past week can be rebuilt exactly.
  • Moves change the path, not the bytes. At month and year rollover, deltas and monthlies move into deltas/YYYY-MM/ and archive/YYYY/. Their bytes, names and sha256 stay the same.

1.1.0 is GA (maintainer decision, 2026-10-21). The ADR 0004 §5 initial-stabilization period, which ADR 0006 extended to cover the 1.1.0 reorganization, ends with 1.1.0: the in-place exception is closed, published files are immutable from 1.1.0, and every later breaking change opens a new MAJOR line (v2/) under the ADR 0004 MAJOR-line and RFC process.

(b) A breaking change opens a new line¶

A breaking schema change after 1.1.0 opens a new line, v2/2.0/ (ADR 0009 §1), built in full (every monthly full and every delta in the window being republished) alongside /v1/. The old line is then frozen at the switch-over: no new releases, and its files stay downloadable and unchanged. It carries a switch-over notice:

  • in its index.json and in its entry in the root manifest's versions: "status": "frozen" and "superseded_by" naming the line that replaced it (for example "v2/2.0"), as v1/1.0 already carries "superseded_by": "v1/1.1";
  • in its MANIFEST.json and README.txt (c);
  • on the email list and in GitHub Discussions Announcements.

Decided 2026-10-21 by the maintainer: a superseded line is frozen at the switch-over and kept indefinitely. Its status is frozen; there is no eol date, no deprecated status and no deletion window. This amends ADR 0004 §3: the prior line does not stay live for a 6-month window, and the root manifest carries no EOL date. Only one line is current at a time; frozen lines accumulate.

(c) A replaced generation is kept as a frozen line folder¶

Amended by ADR 0009. Each version line has its own folder, v{major}/{major}.{minor}/. A republish that replaces a line's bundles (a new MINOR or MAJOR) publishes into a new folder, and the folder it replaces is frozen in place and kept indefinitely (b): nothing is added, moved or deleted, and it is listed from the root manifest with its status. A frozen folder carries:

  • a MANIFEST.json listing every file with its size and sha256;
  • a README.txt saying what the generation is, which release replaced it and why.

The 1.0.1 generation, first copied to archive/1.0.1/ on 2026-10-18, moves to v1/1.0/. There is no root archive/<version>/ folder any more: archive/YYYY/ now means the prior-year archive inside a line (v1/1.1/archive/YYYY/, monthlies plus deltas/YYYY-MM/). A content-only correction that must rewrite bundles is published the same way, as a new MINOR line folder; a PATCH only adds new dated files (ADR 0009 §5, decided 2026-10-21).

(d) Every manifest carries sha256 per file¶

Every manifest that lists a bundle (the per-line index.json, each delta manifest and each frozen line's MANIFEST.json) gives its size and sha256, and every bundle has a <file>.sha256 sidecar. A user can then tell which generation a local copy belongs to by hashing it, without relying on dates or file names.

(e) Parquet is derived, not a record¶

Parquet is a convenience view of a bundle, not a record:

  • The docs and the manifests label it "derived from the bundle; may be regenerated".
  • Amended by ADR 0009. One Parquet dataset is published per line, at v1/1.1/parquet/, rebuilt from the newest release and overwritten every release. parquet/manifest.json beside it gives derived: true, the build_date, the source_release and dataset, the builder and table_spec_version (the tool version), the schema_version, and each file's size and sha256. There is no Parquet in archive/YYYY/, in frozen line folders (v1/1.0/ has none) or per delta. This reverses the 2026-10-18 decision to keep one Parquet set per archived generation.
  • A versioned Parquet rebuild tool, clinvar-gkm-parquet (planned), builds the Parquet tables from a published bundle. It is versioned on its own cadence, declares which bundle schema_versions it accepts, and the pipeline pins a version of it (ADR 0007, Workstream 1), so a user can rebuild the Parquet of any monthly with the tool version that matches.
  • Caveat: the 1.0.x Parquet was produced by BigQuery SQL from the dict tables, not from the bundle, so it cannot be reproduced bit-for-bit. So is the 1.1.0 Parquet, since Workstream 1 starts after 1.1.0 ships. The tool can rebuild the content of those sets from their bundles, not their bytes.

(f) Download metrics decide how far to go¶

A daily collector stores per-path request counts, bytes served and distinct-client counts for data.clinvar-gkm.org (no IP addresses). Estimated complete downloads of a bundle = bytes served ÷ its size.

Retention and the shape of a switch-over are no longer metrics questions: frozen lines and archive/YYYY/ are kept indefinitely, and a breaking change freezes the old line at the switch-over (b). The metrics decide how far to go beyond that:

  • Promote a frozen line or monthly if it shows N or more estimated complete downloads in a calendar month: consider a citable copy (for example a DOI) for it.

N is the maintainer's decision. The first review is 60 days after collection starts (plan, task 7).

(g) Announcements¶

An advance notice before a republish or a switch-over is required once the email list has subscribers or after GA, whichever comes first. With 1.1.0 GA, every later republish into a new line and every switch-over gets an advance notice. 1.1.0 itself used a completion notice only (changelog, GitHub Release notes, Discussions); this amends the advance-notice requirement in ADR 0006's consequences.

Consequences¶

  • Storage. One frozen generation of bundles is about 24 GB (19 dated bundles, 19.3 GB, plus the 00-latest copies, 4.8 GB): about $0.36 a month at R2's $0.015/GB-month. Frozen lines are kept indefinitely, so this grows by one generation per new line. Only one Parquet dataset exists under (e), so dropping the dated Parquet sets saves about 65 GB. Weekly fulls add at most about 24 GB at the end of a month (ADR 0009). A v2/2.0/ line adds a full live line beside the frozen v1/ lines.
  • Operations. Each republish that replaces a line gains steps: freeze the old line folder, write and verify its MANIFEST.json, write its README.txt. Month and year rollovers move and delete files, and each move or delete is followed by a CDN purge of the old paths (ADR 0009). The uploaders and generate-r2-index.sh publish sha256, status / superseded_by, each monthly's source_release and the frozen lines in the root manifest (built in r2_layout.py, PR #182). A daily GitHub Actions job collects the download stats (secrets CF_ANALYTICS_TOKEN, CF_ZONE_ID); a missed day can be collected again only within the 31-day retention.
  • For users. A monthly or delta file keeps the same bytes and name, though its folder changes at month and year rollover; a weekly full is gone shortly after its month, and is rebuilt from a monthly plus deltas; a superseded generation stays available indefinitely as a frozen line folder (v1/1.0/); a checksum tells them which generation they hold; a frozen line says what replaced it; and they can rebuild Parquet from any monthly with clinvar-gkm-parquet.
  • Metric limits. Cloudflare's adaptive sampling makes counts estimates. Distinct clients are counted per day by IP, so NAT undercounts and roaming clients overcount. Bytes ÷ size counts two half downloads as one. Traffic before 2026-10-14 is not measurable.
  • Harder: from 1.1.0 (GA), a mistake in a published bundle can no longer be fixed quietly. It needs a new MINOR line (content-only, ADR 0009 §5) or a new MAJOR line (breaking), with an advance notice (g).
