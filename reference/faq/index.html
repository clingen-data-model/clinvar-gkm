FAQ¶

Answers to recurring questions about how ClinVar content appears in ClinVar-GKM. Each answer states where the behavior comes from in the pipeline and how to see it in the data.

Where does an SCV proposition's gene context come from?¶

The gene in an SCV proposition's geneContextQualifier always comes from the variant, never from the submitter. The submitter's own gene symbols are kept alongside it, in the submittedGeneSymbols extension. From 1.1.0, a geneContextSource extension says where the gene came from.

How the qualifier is built¶

Step 2 of gkm_scv_statement_proc (see Propositions) builds the qualifier as follows:

  • The gene is the variant's single representative gene, taken from the upstream single_gene_variation table (built by clinvar-ingest-bq-tools). That table picks at most one gene per variant by rules applied in order, strongest evidence first: the gene named in the variant's own name (a MANE transcript, an HGVS-style name or a star allele), then ClinVar's gene associations, then the shared gene of a haplotype's or genotype's alleles. Several rules are calculated upstream rather than supplied by ClinVar. See the Single Gene Variation method for the rules, the reasons for them and their known limitations.
  • The gene sets the qualifier's name (the gene symbol), its primaryCoding (the NCBI Gene id) and its HGNC mappings. The symbol and HGNC id are the ones the upstream method used for that release, so they come with the gene, even for a gene ClinVar names in the variant title without associating it.
  • The submitter's genes are the symbols the submitter gave in their submitted variant record. They are attached to the qualifier as the submittedGeneSymbols extension, verbatim and in submitted order. They never choose the gene.
  • The source is the geneContextSource extension, on every qualifier from 1.1.0:
    • submittedSymbolMatches: the submitter's symbols include the qualifier's symbol
    • submittedSymbolDiffers: the submitter gave symbols, but none equals the qualifier's symbol
    • inferredFromVariant: the submitter gave no symbols
    • submittedNotNormalized: the placeholder (next item)

Symbols are compared after trimming spaces and ignoring case, so C19ORF12 matches C19orf12. The schema accepts only these four values. - The placeholder applies when the variant has no single representative gene but the submitter named one. From 1.1.0, the qualifier's name is the submitter's symbol, verbatim; several symbols are joined with , in submitted order. It has no primaryCoding, the submitter's symbols are in submittedGeneSymbols, and geneContextSource is submittedNotNormalized. The 1.0.1 releases name it with the literal text submitted genes were not normalized. - When neither source has a gene, the proposition has no geneContextQualifier.

VCV and RCV propositions carry no gene context qualifier.

For example, SCV000024718-PATH has a qualifier gene of GBA1 (NCBI Gene 2629), while the submitter wrote GBA, the gene's previous symbol. The symbols differ, but the gene is the same:

"geneContextQualifier": {
  "type": "MappableConcept",
  "conceptType": "gene",
  "name": "GBA1",
  "primaryCoding": {
    "code": "2629",
    "name": "GBA1",
    "system": "https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/",
    "iris": ["https://identifiers.org/ncbigene:2629", "https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/2629"]
  },
  "mappings": [
    {
      "coding": {
        "code": "4177",
        "system": "https://www.genenames.org",
        "iris": ["https://identifiers.org/hgnc:4177", "https://www.genenames.org/data/gene-symbol-report/#!/hgnc_id/4177"]
      },
      "relation": "exactMatch"
    }
  ],
  "extensions": [
    { "name": "submittedGeneSymbols", "value": ["GBA"] },
    { "name": "geneContextSource", "value": "submittedSymbolDiffers" }
  ]
}

How the two sources compare¶

Across the 6.74 million SCV propositions in the 1.0.1 2026-10 monthly (built from the 2026-09-28 release and frozen under v1/1.0/), the last release before geneContextSource, with symbols compared exactly:

Case Propositions Share
The submitter's gene is the qualifier gene 5,781,673 85.8%
The submitter gave the qualifier gene plus others 101 <0.1%
The submitter's gene differs from the qualifier gene 27,817 0.4%
The gene is inferred from the variant; the submitter gave none 881,325 13.1%
Placeholder: the submitter gave a gene, the variant has no single gene 4,814 0.1%
No gene from either source 40,849 0.6%

The 27,817 differing cases break down as follows:

Kind of difference Propositions Example
The submitted symbol is not a gene symbol in the release's gene list — mostly a previous symbol of the same gene (GBA → GBA1, MUT → MMUT, C10orf2 → TWNK, IKBKAP → ELP1) 24,545 SCV000024718-PATH
The submitted gene is another gene ClinVar associates with the variant 2,541 SCV000425435-PATH: ITGA4 vs submitted CERKL
The submitted gene is a gene ClinVar does not associate with the variant 731 SCV004053541-PATH: PRAMEF18 vs submitted PRAMEF22

The two sources therefore agree almost everywhere. In the 1.0.1 releases (frozen under v1/1.0/), three gaps remained:

  • Inferred genes were not marked. For 881,325 propositions the qualifier states a gene the submitter never stated, and nothing in the record distinguished an inferred gene from a submitted one. You had to compare name with submittedGeneSymbols: when the extension is absent, the gene is inferred.
  • Placeholder names were not genes. For the 4,814 placeholder propositions, the submitter's gene was only in submittedGeneSymbols. Grouping or filtering on the qualifier name treated submitted genes were not normalized as if it were a gene.
  • A differing symbol is usually a renamed gene. Matching the submitter's symbol to the qualifier's symbol by string comparison over-reports disagreement. Only the last two rows of the table above are different genes.

From 1.1.0, the first two gaps are closed and the third is labeled:

  • Inferred genes are marked geneContextSource = inferredFromVariant.
  • Placeholder names carry the submitter's symbol and are marked submittedNotNormalized. A placeholder is still not a normalized gene, so filter on primaryCoding (or the Parquet gene_context_id) when you need one. Filtering on name alone now also matches placeholders: 29 TP53 placeholders in 2026-10-18, for example.
  • submittedSymbolDiffers says only that the symbols differ, not that the genes do. With the case-insensitive comparison it covers 28,507 propositions in 2026-10-18, where an exact comparison finds 28,653. The other 146 differ only by case and are now submittedSymbolMatches.

How to see it in the data¶

The qualifier is part of each SCV proposition in the bundle and in the data column of the proposition Parquet tables:

-- Extensions are picked by name: the qualifier can carry two. (`lambda e:` needs DuckDB 1.3 or later.)
SELECT id,
       data->>'$.geneContextQualifier.name'               AS qualifier_gene,
       data->>'$.geneContextQualifier.primaryCoding.code' AS ncbi_gene,
       list_filter(CAST(data->'$.geneContextQualifier.extensions' AS JSON[]),
                   lambda e: e->>'$.name' = 'submittedGeneSymbols')[1]->'$.value' AS submitted_gene_symbols,
       list_filter(CAST(data->'$.geneContextQualifier.extensions' AS JSON[]),
                   lambda e: e->>'$.name' = 'geneContextSource')[1]->>'$.value'  AS gene_context_source
FROM 'propositionPathogenicity.parquet'
WHERE id IN ('SCV001494816-PATH', 'SCV000024718-PATH', 'SCV005419060-PATH', 'SCV000039919-PATH');

With 1.1.0 data:

id                 qualifier_gene  ncbi_gene  submitted_gene_symbols  gene_context_source
SCV005419060-PATH  TTN             7273       ["TTN","TTN-AS1"]       submittedSymbolMatches
SCV001494816-PATH  KCNT1           NULL       ["KCNT1"]               submittedNotNormalized
SCV000024718-PATH  GBA1            2629       ["GBA"]                 submittedSymbolDiffers
SCV000039919-PATH  SERPINC1        462        NULL                    inferredFromVariant

In the 1.0.1 releases (frozen under v1/1.0/), SCV001494816-PATH is named submitted genes were not normalized and has no geneContextSource.

From 1.1.0, the four proposition Parquet tables also carry typed gene_context_id (the qualifier's ncbigene: id, NULL for the placeholder), submitted_gene_symbols and gene_context_source columns.

Gene context is not the variant's gene list¶

The qualifier holds one representative gene. A variant's full set of ClinVar gene associations — every gene it lies in, overlaps or is near, each with a relationship type — is a different relationship. It is carried on the variant as the clinvarGeneList extension (see GeneListItem). Use the gene list to find all variants touching a gene, and the gene context qualifier for the gene a classification is framed around.
