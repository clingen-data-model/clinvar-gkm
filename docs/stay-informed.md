# Stay Informed

ClinVar-GKM announces releases, version changes, and events on an email list, the Google Group
**[clinvar-gkm@broadinstitute.org](https://groups.google.com/a/broadinstitute.org/g/clinvar-gkm)**. Anyone can join,
and you don't need a Broad Institute or GitHub account.

---

## What the list announces

- **Each monthly release.** When a new [monthly full bundle](data-access/monthly-full.md) is published, an
  automatic message gives its link and checksum and a digest of the
  [weekly deltas](data-access/weekly-deltas.md) since the previous monthly: each one's date, the records it
  added, updated and deleted, and a direct link. The same post appears in the repository's
  [Discussions → Announcements](https://github.com/clingen-data-model/clinvar-gkm/discussions/categories/announcements).
  Individual weekly releases aren't announced; follow the line's Atom feed, `feed.xml` (see [Downloads](data-access/download.md)), for those.
- **New versions.** When a new version is ready (for example 1.1.0), with what changed and links to the
  [Changelog](reference/changelog.md) and the downloads.
- **Version changes, in advance.** Before any breaking change, the list carries the proposal (a Request for
  Comment) and its comment window. Before a republish into a new version line, it carries a notice with the
  switch-over date. See [Versioning & Releases](reference/versioning.md#stay-informed-have-a-say).
- **Corrections and known issues** that affect published data. The current list is on
  [Known Issues](reference/known-issues.md).
- **Events and presentations** where ClinVar-GKM is featured, such as conference talks, workshops and webinars.
  Past decks are on [Presentations](reference/presentations/index.md).

## Join the list

**With a Google account.** A Google account can use any email address; it doesn't have to be Gmail.

1. Open the group page: [groups.google.com/a/broadinstitute.org/g/clinvar-gkm](https://groups.google.com/a/broadinstitute.org/g/clinvar-gkm).
2. Sign in, then select **Join group**.
3. Choose how you receive messages: every email, a daily digest, or web only. Then confirm.

**By email, without a Google account.**

1. Send an email, with any subject and body, to **clinvar-gkm+subscribe@broadinstitute.org**.
2. Reply to, or follow the link in, the confirmation message that comes back.

**To leave,** select **Leave group** on the group page, or send an email to
**clinvar-gkm+unsubscribe@broadinstitute.org**.

## Other ways to follow along

- **Release feed:** each version line publishes an Atom feed, for example
  `https://data.clinvar-gkm.org/v1/1.1/feed.xml`, which any feed reader can follow. It lists every published
  release, including each weekly update.
- **GitHub:** watch the [repository](https://github.com/clingen-data-model/clinvar-gkm). Choose **Custom**, then
  **Releases** and **Discussions**, to get tagged releases and change proposals.
- **Questions and feedback:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/clingen-data-model/clinvar-gkm/discussions) for
  questions and ideas, and [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/clingen-data-model/clinvar-gkm/issues) for problems with
  the data.
